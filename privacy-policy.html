Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026 — Fresh Homemade Foods, Dubai Town, Raiwind Road, Lahore, Pakistan.

Fresh Homemade Foods ("we", "us", "our") runs a homemade food delivery service in Lahore. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit our website or order food from us, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using this website, you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

We collect only what we need to take and deliver your order:

  • Order details you give us on WhatsApp: your name, phone number, delivery address, the dishes you order, and any delivery instructions or allergy notes you share.
  • Messages: the messages you send us on WhatsApp about orders, monthly mess plans, feedback or complaints.
  • Website usage data: basic, anonymous information such as pages visited, approximate location (city level), device type and browser. This comes from standard web analytics and helps us see which dishes and pages visitors like most. It does not identify you by name.

We do not run user accounts on this website, we do not ask for your CNIC, bank card or bank account details, and we never ask for your password for any service. Payment is cash on delivery.

2. How we use your information

  • To confirm your order, cook it, pack it and deliver it to your address on time.
  • To contact you about your order — for example, if a dish is sold out, if we need directions, or if delivery will be late.
  • To arrange monthly mess plans for students and job holders.
  • To respond to your questions, feedback and complaints, and to improve our menu and service.
  • To meet legal obligations, for example keeping a record of orders for our own accounts.

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information to anyone.

3. Who we share information with

We share your order details only with the people who need them to fulfil your order — our home kitchen team who cooks and packs the food, and the rider who delivers it. They see your name, address and phone number for that delivery only.

We also use the following third-party services, which have their own privacy policies:

  • WhatsApp (Meta): when you tap an order button, you leave our website and message us on WhatsApp. Your messages are handled under WhatsApp's privacy policy.
  • Google AdSense and Google Analytics: see Sections 4 and 5 below.
  • Vercel: our website hosting provider, which serves this website to you.

4. Cookies and Google AdSense

This website uses cookies — small files stored on your device — to make the site work and to show advertising.

Google AdSense is the advertising service we use. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and other sites on the internet. Google uses the DoubleClick cookie (DART cookie) for this purpose. You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, and you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalised advertising by visiting aboutads.info/choices.

Our website does not set advertising cookies itself. You can delete or block cookies in your browser settings at any time; the website and the menu will still work, although some features (such as remembering your menu search) may not.

5. Google Analytics

We may use Google Analytics to understand, in aggregate, how many people visit the site, which dishes are viewed most, and which cities visitors come from. This data is anonymous and is not combined with your name, phone number or address.

6. How long we keep your information

Order messages on WhatsApp are kept for as long as we need them to run the business — for example, to handle a repeat order, a mess plan, or a complaint about a recent delivery. We delete old order chats when they are no longer needed. Anonymous website statistics are kept in aggregate form.

7. Your rights

You can ask us at any time to:

  • Tell you what order information we hold about you.
  • Correct your name, phone number or address.
  • Delete your order messages and stop contacting you about offers, except where we must keep a record for legal or accounting reasons.

Just message us on WhatsApp at 0303 4438788 and we will help. We will never charge you for this.

8. Children's privacy

This website is intended for adults and for students ordering food for themselves. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent and believe your child has shared personal information with us, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information: order details are handled only by our small kitchen and delivery team, and we do not store card or bank details anywhere. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, so please do not send us sensitive information such as passwords or bank details over WhatsApp.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time — for example, if we add online payment or a new delivery partner. The "Last updated" date at the top will change, and the new version will apply from the day it is published on this page.
