6. Refunds and food quality

We cook every dish fresh and pack it sealed. If your food arrives cold, damaged, wrong, or you find a genuine quality problem, tell us on WhatsApp within 1 hour of delivery, with a photo if possible. Refunds, where agreed, are paid in cash on your next delivery or by the method we agree with you. We do not refund food that was eaten in full, or orders cancelled after cooking started.
