What to send us when you order

  • The dish name and quantity (for example: "1 Biryani Medium, 1 Chicken Karahi").
  • Your full delivery address, with a hostel, office or house name and a nearby landmark.
  • Your phone number, if you are messaging from a different number.
  • Any allergy or spice note (for example: "less spicy", "no nuts").

We will confirm the price, any delivery charge, and the delivery time before we start cooking.

Monthly mess and group orders

For a monthly mess plan (lunch only, or lunch + dinner) or a group order for a hostel, office or gathering of 5 or more people, message us on WhatsApp at least one day in advance. Tell us how many people, lunch or dinner, and your address, and we will send you the plan and price.

Complaints and feedback

If your food arrives late, cold, wrong or you find a quality problem, please tell us on WhatsApp within 1 hour of delivery, with a photo if you can. We will replace the dish, discount your next order, or refund that dish — see our Terms of Service. Honest feedback helps us cook better, so please do tell us.
