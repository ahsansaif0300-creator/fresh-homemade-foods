About Us

Fresh Homemade Foods — Dubai Town, Raiwind Road, Lahore.

Fresh Homemade Foods is a home kitchen, not a restaurant. We cook the food we would cook for our own family — fresh, every day, in a clean home kitchen — and deliver it hot to your doorstep in Lahore.

The idea is simple. Thousands of students and job holders live away from home in Lahore — in hostels, shared rooms and offices around Raiwind Road, Bahria Town, Lake City and nearby areas. They miss ghar ka khana: real Biryani, Chicken Karahi, Qorma, Daal Chawal, Channa Pulao and Haleem, made the home way, not bazaar food cooked in old oil and served cold. That is the gap we fill.

How it works

  • You order on WhatsApp — pick a dish from our 70-dish homemade menu, or ask for a daily tiffin or a monthly mess plan.
  • We cook fresh — our home food-preparing team cooks your dish fresh after your order, with fresh ingredients, clean utensils and gloves.
  • We pack it sealed and deliver it hot to your home, hostel or office, usually within the lunch (12–3 PM) or dinner (7–10 PM) window.
  • You pay cash on delivery. No app, no account, no card needed.

What we stand for

🧼 Hygiene first

Clean kitchen, fresh ingredients, gloves and clean utensils, sealed packing. No preservatives and no stale food — if it is not fresh, we do not send it.

🍛 Home taste

Desi recipes the home way: slow-cooked Haleem, rich Qorma, soft Channa Pulao, fresh Paratha and Doodh Patti. The taste of home, not of a food street.

🎓 Made for students & job holders

Affordable prices (from Rs 28 for a roti), filling tiffins, and monthly mess plans so you never have to decide lunch and dinner every day.

⏰ On time

Fixed lunch and dinner windows, confirmed on WhatsApp, and an honest message if we are ever running late.

Prices start at Rs 28 and the full menu is on the homepage. We only serve desi homemade food: no ice cream, no packaged drinks, no pizza.
